# Living Table First Steps 0.1.10

Quest 3 chess test build, version code 11. Self-hosted vision host 0.4.1 remains compatible; no new service or paid dependency.

## Test route
1. NEW GAME → REAL BOARD. Select the four **outer playing-square corners**: near-left, near-right, far-right, far-left. Exclude the frame; sit behind your chosen color.
2. Place the king guide over your king's square and pinch/pull vertically or use KING +/− to size the whole set. Press SIZE READY.
3. Read/review the scanned pieces. Correct uncertain squares or rescan. Select whose turn it is and known castling/en-passant rights. Press the large purple **START GAME** button.
4. Move your real piece and CHECK BOARD. Check the virtual preview and ACCEPT MOVE. History must advance once.
5. The AI ghost previews its move. Move the real AI piece (and rook/captured piece if prompted), then CHECK BOARD. Correct alignment must return YOUR TURN; a wrong/missing piece must stay blocked with a message.
6. Move your head around the table: grid and pieces must stay registered together. Move the real board only after selecting realignment. Test room/recenter recovery separately.
7. Try CANCEL SCAN and the persistent menu. Verify that history stays unchanged and START GAME is always a deliberate final action. Resize the backdrop with its menu slider.

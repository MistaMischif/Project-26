# Living Table 0.6.2 — Center-board character celebration

Player pawns retain their original starting scale during movement, landing and session restoration. Shared-space pieces are spaced without shrinking.

A special-card illustration lifts off its card and holds above the center of the board while the named card flies to the discard pile in two seconds. Character-colored sparkles explode behind the illustration, spread outward and arc to the legal destination square. The illustration then dissolves into sparkles. The status panel stays hidden during this celebration.

The detached effect follows board relocation, respects pause, and is cleaned up when a card/game is cleared. Restored discard cards do not replay celebrations. No character sculptures or character voices are restored.

Use Candy Land setup -> TEST SPECIAL CARDS, followed by REPLAY SPECIAL or NEXT SPECIAL. This sandbox does not change saved games.

Desktop render/runtime tests and Quest startup checks are recorded in VALIDATION.txt. Headset appearance still needs user review.
